FAQ

How do I play? Sit in a circle and pass the phone. Tap the deck to draw a card and do what it says before the timer runs out. When time is up, your group decides the penalty together. Press Continue for the next card until the deck is finished.

Who decides the penalties? You do! The game only suggests fun ideas like singing, dancing or squats. Keep it friendly and safe.

How many cards are there? 52 cards: 13 card types, 4 of each, shuffled every game.

Ads and tracking. The game is free and shows ads. You can decline personalized ads in the tracking prompt or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

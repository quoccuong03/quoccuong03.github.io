FAQ

How do I play? Tap a car to drive it in the direction of its arrow. If the path is clear it leaves the lot and boards the truck of the same color. Cars of other colors wait in the parking spots — when all spots are full, the level is lost.

A car won't move. Another car is in its way. Clear the blocking car first.

Can I get an extra spot? When you run out of space you can watch a short video to get one more waiting spot.

Is my progress saved? Yes, your current level is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Ads and tracking. The game is free and shows ads. You can decline personalized ads in the tracking prompt or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

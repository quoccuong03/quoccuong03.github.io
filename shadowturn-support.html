FAQ

How do I play? Drag on the screen to turn the object under the lamp. When its shadow fits the glowing outline on the wall, the shadow turns into a picture and the level is solved.

I'm stuck! Tap the light bulb for a hint: the object turns most of the way to the answer. You get a few free hints; after that you can watch a short video for another one.

Where are my pictures? Every solved shadow is saved in your Shadow Album on the home screen. Progress is stored on your device only.

Ads and tracking. The game is free and shows ads. You can decline personalized ads in the tracking prompt or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

FAQ

How do I play? Tap Decorate, pick a piece of furniture and drag it anywhere in the room. Little friends from the valley drop by to use the things they love. Tap a friend to say hi.

How do I meet a new friend? Each friend loves certain furniture. Put it in your home and the friend will visit soon. Your album shows hints for friends you haven't met yet.

How do I become better friends? Say hi every time a friend visits. Each friendship level brings a handwritten letter, and at level 3 a keepsake only that friend gives.

How do I open the Lotus Pond and Stony Brook? Collect golden leaves: you get one for every new friend and friendship level, and two for a finished album. Open the map to travel.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Ads and tracking. The game is free. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a bonus, plus an occasional short ad between moments (never in your first days). You can decline personalized ads in the tracking prompt, or later in your phone's privacy settings.
